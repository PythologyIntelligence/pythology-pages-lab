OUTCOME CUSTODY

No selective success record

The ledger's reported calibration record includes 45 observed outcomes and 101 not-observed outcomes among its 146 resolved forecasts. These labels must retain their precise ledger definitions and must not be repurposed as binary geological forecasting skill.

EVALUATION BOUNDARY

A separate research pathway

The public forecast ledger is distinct from the offline Unified Research Engine V3 baseline. It does not by itself validate earthquake forecasts, scientific discovery or cross-domain predictive performance.

INVESTIGATE · FREEZE · SCORE

Recorded before the answer is known.

01 · Register

Define the question, forecast, probability, evidence timestamp and evaluation window.

02 · Preserve

Retain source records and competing explanations rather than revising history after an event.

03 · Resolve

Apply the recorded rules to later outcomes, including inconclusive and missed observations.

04 · Evaluate

Measure reliability, calibration, base rates and false alarms using comparable questions.
