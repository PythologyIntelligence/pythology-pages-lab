ENERGY · ILLUSTRATIVE APPLICATION

Wind-turbine condition and sensor histories

The schematic shows how vibration, loading, temperature, operating state and maintenance records could be compared with ordinary behaviour to investigate possible changes before faults. A real study would require authorised telemetry and known outcomes.

Evidence boundary: Visual concept only — no live turbine feed or demonstrated fault forecasting.
INFRASTRUCTURE · WORLD MODELS

Bridges, structures and physics-constrained simulation

Investigate how geometry, materials, boundary conditions, weather loads and sensor observations could be combined into bounded digital models. Simulated departures require independent solver, field and inspection evidence.

Evidence boundary: Exploratory engineering research; not certified structural monitoring.
DECISIONS · TESTING

Compare maintenance or intervention choices

The long-term decision-research workflow would freeze an evidence state, declare baseline and alternative interventions, run constrained simulations, and later compare recorded expectations with actual outcomes. False alarms and misses must stay on record.

Evidence boundary: Conditional simulations must not be represented as verified physical outcomes.

The physics-informed research programme is distinct from operational monitoring or engineering certification. The diagrams are conceptual and do not contain live asset data.

PROSPECTIVE DESIGN · NOT FIELD PROOF

Freeze decisions and compare outcomes

Proposed decision-futures studies compare a frozen baseline with a small number of clearly defined interventions. Evidence must later show which action occurred and what happened, including misses and uncertainty.

Simulation branches are not measurements or validated outcomes.
