HELIOS + CHATHAMS ECOWATCH

Two projects. One community.

01 · COMMUNITY WEATHER

Helios — Chatham Islands

A practical weather-information interface designed for the remote island community. It presents forecasts and atmospheric context, without claiming to replace official forecasts or emergency alerts.

02 · ECOLOGICAL MONITORING

Chathams EcoWatch

A satellite-derived ecological and environmental monitoring interface for baselines and changes worth investigating. An unusual pattern is not independently verified evidence of species loss or ecological harm.

FROM RĒKOHU TO MAINLAND AOTEAROA

A local need can reveal a wider problem.

Helios makes regional weather information easier to access; EcoWatch organises ecological observations into a view where environmental questions can be investigated. Extending similar work to mainland New Zealand would require appropriate scope, local data coverage, independent review and stakeholder involvement.

COMMUNITY BENEFIT

CONSERVATION QUESTIONS

Explore meaningful environmental change

Use satellite-derived evidence cautiously. A visual difference is a candidate for investigation, not proof of ecosystem damage.
