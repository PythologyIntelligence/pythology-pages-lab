01 / COMMUNITY WEATHER

Helios.
Weather that meets people where they are.

When concerns about access to dedicated weather information for the Chatham Islands were raised, we built a weather intelligence service and made it freely accessible to the community.

Helios puts changing conditions, forecast periods and weather context into an interface people can actually use. It is a living weather service, not a claim that Pythology has replaced official warning authorities.

02 / ECOLOGICAL INTELLIGENCE

Chathams EcoWatch.
Change you can investigate.

EcoWatch was developed for conservation monitoring needs on the Chatham Islands, including potential use by the Department of Conservation. It uses environmental observations and satellite-derived measurements to investigate changes over time.

Vegetation condition, moisture and water-related indices can be compared with baselines so unusual differences are visible to reviewers, rather than disappearing into a collection of disconnected charts.

Copernicus satellite evidenceBaselinesAnomaly review

Research and pilot-stage monitoring; satellite indices are indicators to investigate, not verified species counts or proof of ecosystem damage. This work is not presented as an official DOC endorsement or contract.

WHAT COMES NEXT · PROPOSED

From Rēkohu
to mainland Aotearoa.

One regional deployment is a proving ground for a framework that could support conservation across New Zealand.

The mainland programme is a proposal, not a completed deployment or a DOC commitment. Each region would require suitable sources, local baselines, ground-truth checks and the right operational partners.
