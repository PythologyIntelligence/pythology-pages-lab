What gets brought together?

Separate evidence families, one readable state.

  • Seismic: catalogue events, magnitude, depth, reviewed status and spatial concentrations.
  • Volcanic: official status and guarded measurement changes against their own recent baselines.
  • Weather: incoming rainfall, heat, wind and other developing atmospheric pressure.
  • Ocean: regional marine context, temperature and current conditions where relevant.
  • Data quality: stale, failed or missing sources remain visible as unknown rather than being silently treated as normal.
Research and operational context only. GeoNet, MetService, NEMA, councils and other responsible authorities remain authoritative for New Zealand public hazard and safety information.

Underneath EarthNet

Each part gets one job.

The machinery underneath can be sophisticated. The public explanation does not need to be. Keeping these jobs separate is what stops a sensor reading from quietly becoming a causal claim or a scenario from pretending to be a fact.

EARTHNET

What is happening?

Acquire, normalise and organise changing real-world evidence into a current state.

EVIDENCE

What do we actually know?

Keep source, age, contradiction, missing information and observation quality visible.

CAUSAL

What might connect it?

Compare mechanisms and competing explanations instead of promoting association into causation.

PROMETHEUS

What should happen next?

Commit testable forecasts before outcomes are known, then resolve and calibrate them later.

DECISION FUTURES

What changes if the choice changes?

Compare conditional branches from the same evidence without turning simulated detail into certainty.

SENTINEL

Can a human challenge the chain?

Interrogate evidence, reasoning, degraded sources, uncertainty and the assumptions carrying a conclusion.

EarthNet and Prometheus are research and decision-support systems in live validation. They are not official warning services, emergency instructions or replacements for responsible authorities and domain experts.

A forecast is not a decision

Prometheus tells you what might happen. Decision Futures tells you what changes if you act — and what happens if you don't.

When EarthNet surfaces a compound-risk situation with a live Prometheus forecast and plausible human consequence, it opens a Decision Futures candidate. The candidate pre-declares the decision class and the branches — for example, maintain current posture vs. escalate readiness vs. stage resources earlier — before the outcome is known.

If an authorised human opens the trial, both branches are tracked. When the outcome resolves, you see not just whether the forecast was right, but whether the decision changed anything. The counterfactual is preserved. The choice is scored.
