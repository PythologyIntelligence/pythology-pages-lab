Pythology in our own words

Purpose, scientific ambition
and engineering integrity.

Our work is motivated by useful intelligence, the curiosity to understand what causes change, and the discipline to leave an honest research record behind. Commercial success would let us sustain and expand that work.

01 / THE FOUNDATION

Build intelligence that can be challenged.

The Causal Rust Core evaluates explicitly declared, evidence-linked mechanisms with reproducible traces. EarthNet, Atlas, Poseidon, ARCUS and the wider architecture give specialist observations and context their own roles. A traceable calculation is not proof that the mechanism is correct.

Explore the architecture →
02 / THE RESEARCH

Keep the misses.

Prometheus records prospective forecasts, uncertainty and competing explanations, then returns when reality provides an outcome. We want to learn from both successes and failures, and test mechanisms rather than merely celebrate a prediction that happened to land.

See the public record →
03 / COMMUNITY

Make useful technology accessible.

Helios Chathams is our location-aware weather and ocean information project for the islands. A broader Chathams Environmental System is planned as pro bono work, with satellite-derived environmental context and features shaped by actual local needs. Proposed work is not yet delivered work.

Discuss the Chathams work →
04 / CONNECTIONS

Oracle and Nemesis are joining the picture.

Oracle is our internal geospatial query and situational-awareness direction. Nemesis is a developing global event and context surface. They are integration-stage components: intended to strengthen the wider intelligence environment, not claimed as fully connected services today.

Explore the stack →
05 / AGENT RESEARCH

A little society with enormous possibilities.

Félagskapr is a Norse-inspired simulated world of agents with needs, memories and choices. As its behaviour, measurements and visual presentation mature, it could become a visible laboratory for agent evaluation and Decision Futures experiments. Simulated outcomes are not claims about real human societies.

See our research direction →
06 / INVESTMENT AND PARTNERSHIP

Examine the opportunity, and the risks.

Our proposition is a reusable technical foundation, transparent scientific research and possible specialist applications. We are early-stage, and prospective customers, research validity, operating costs and commercial adoption still deserve scrutiny. Brent handles real investment discussions directly.
