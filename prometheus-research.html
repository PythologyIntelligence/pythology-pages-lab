MEET PROMETHEUS · RESEARCH ENGINE

Investigate. Test. Remember.

Prometheus is the developing research engine connecting observations, historical evidence and testable hypotheses. Its work must remain open to challenge: a candidate signal is not a forecast, and a forecast is not a warning.

WHAT THE ENGINE DOES

A research process that can be questioned.

One method should make decisions, uncertainty and failures easier to inspect — not more difficult to see.

01

Observe and preserve

Capture timestamped observations and source-linked historical context. The evidence must remain traceable to its origin.

02

Investigate alternatives

Ask whether a pattern differs from ordinary background conditions. Search for competing explanations and contradictory evidence.

03

Record the outcome

Write the hypothesis before an outcome is known. Keep missed events, false alarms and inconclusive cases on the record.

INVESTIGATE · TEST · REMEMBER

How we approach the evidence

01 / CAPTURE

Source-linked evidence

Retain references, timestamps, methods and the exact input window.

02 / TEST

Controls and contradictions

Compare event and non-event periods without choosing the answer after the fact.

03 / LEARN

Review without rewriting history

Use human-reviewed findings to refine what is worth investigating next.

Research baseline figures and claimed capabilities must be reconciled against the latest verified engineering reports before publication. No reliable earthquake forecasting or autonomous scientific discovery is claimed.
RESEARCH SNAPSHOT · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

A measured starting point is not a scientific result.

What is not yet established

Reliable earthquake forecasting, clinically validated biological detection, autonomous scientific discovery or independently demonstrated adaptive learning. Each claimed capability requires domain-specific external validation.
