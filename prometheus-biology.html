Prometheus · Biological research programme

Questions about life.
Evidence before answers.

One research direction, three initial scientific challenges.

We are organising Pythology's separate biological evidence and multi-omic research prototypes into a unified Prometheus Biology programme. The ambition is to investigate complex biological change with traceable hypotheses and independent testing, not to turn exploratory anomalies into diagnoses.

Prometheus Biology · Exploratory

Three priorities.
One evidence-led programme.

01 / CANCER BIOLOGY

Pancreatic cancer

Exploring whether molecular, transcriptomic and pathway relationships can generate testable questions about early biological transitions. The existing screening prototype is not clinically validated.

02 / NEURODEGENERATION

Alzheimer's disease

Investigating disease-associated biological states, mechanistic hypotheses and uncertainty using appropriately characterised research datasets. No clinical prediction capability has been established.

03 / UNDERSTUDIED CONDITIONS

Rare diseases

Developing structured evidence review and hypothesis workflows for heterogeneous, data-limited disorders. Findings need expert interpretation and independent validation.

Existing foundations

Research prototypes.
Not clinical tools.

Existing experiments include healthy-reference transcriptomic anomaly screening, multi-omic hypothesis organisation and biological evidence-relationship modelling. An anomaly score is not a disease probability, a diagnosis, prognosis or treatment recommendation.

SPECIALIST ARCHITECTURE

Bio-Symbology

Evidence-linked biological mechanisms and precursor-state hypothesis concepts, subject to scientific validation.
