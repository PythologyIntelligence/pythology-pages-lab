The reason behind the research

Because earlier awareness could save lives.

Natural hazards can develop rapidly. Environmental damage can unfold quietly over months or years. In both cases, recognising meaningful changes sooner could create valuable time.

Time for scientists to investigate. Time for communities to prepare. Time for people to make better-informed decisions.

We cannot prevent earthquakes, volcanic eruptions or severe storms. And identifying an unusual pattern does not mean we can predict what happens next.

Our mission is to discover which early signals are genuinely useful — and help turn that understanding into earlier awareness.

Meet Prometheus

Our research observer. Always looking. Always learning.

Prometheus is Pythology's autonomous environmental research system. He examines incoming observations, identifies patterns worth investigating and records what he found — including where and when he found it.

When later events provide new evidence, he can compare those outcomes with his earlier observations.

His purpose isn't to declare that every anomaly predicts an event. It's to help us discover which patterns, if any, consistently provide meaningful advance information.
