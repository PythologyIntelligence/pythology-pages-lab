Understanding change.
Earlier.

We are developing Prometheus to investigate changes in complex systems before their consequences become obvious. His research brings observations, competing explanations and testable evidence together, with a focus on recognising meaningful early signals — and learning when they are not meaningful.

Environmental ↗Earth systems, changing weather, geology and ecology. Research into what happens before significant environmental events.Biological ↗Exploring biological transitions, disease-associated patterns and evidence-linked questions that require independent scientific testing.Physical Systems ↗Investigating sensor evidence from machines, structures and engineered systems — from subtle changes to possible failure pathways.

Prometheus is the common research engine behind this direction. Understand Prometheus and what has been verified.

Early-signal investigations are under scientific evaluation. Pythology does not provide official emergency warnings, clinical guidance, or engineering safety certification.
