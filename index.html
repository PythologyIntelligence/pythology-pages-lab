The problem

Most anomalies mean nothing. A few might.

Every day the Earth, its living systems and our machines pour out measurements. Somewhere in that flood there may be changes that appear before something significant happens. Or there may not.

We don’t assume. We build ways to find out, and we keep the record either way.

A candidate signal is not a forecast.
A forecast is not a warning.

Where it applies

One method. Three kinds of change.

Prometheus is the research engine underneath all of it. Each field brings its own questions, its own experts and its own standard of proof.

Environmental

What was changing before?

Earthquakes and volcanic settings, storms and oceans, slow ecological drift. Every candidate signal is compared against matched quiet periods, so we can see what’s simply ordinary.

For geoscience and climate researchers, conservation teams, councils and agencies.

Explore environmental →
Biological

Where does biology begin to shift?

Pancreatic cancer, Alzheimer’s disease and rare conditions, explored as testable hypotheses. Never as diagnoses. Independent validation comes before any claim.

For biomedical scientists, university labs and disease research groups. Exploratory research, not a clinical service.

Explore biological →
Physical Systems

Can the numbers warn us first?

Sensor readings from bridges, machines and structures, compared with how they normally behave, to see whether the first signs of trouble show up early. Research foundations only, for now.

For engineers, infrastructure owners, manufacturers and aerospace teams. Not certified monitoring.

Meet Prometheus

Investigate. Test. Remember.

A research system that has to show its evidence, and can be questioned.

01
It watches what’s changing

Environmental and geophysical observations flow in, and it notes the shifts and the unusual patterns.

02
It questions the evidence

It compares observations, weighs alternative explanations, and writes down what the evidence supports, uncertainty included.

03
It lets reality answer

Predictions are recorded before the outcome is known. Hits, misses and inconclusive results all stay on the record.
