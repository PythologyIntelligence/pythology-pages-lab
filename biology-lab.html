THE RESEARCH REGISTER

Two missions. Open scientific questions.

These are proposed investigations, not demonstrated breakthroughs. Each mission will need independent evidence, rigorous controls and domain-expert review before its hypotheses can be treated as findings.

MISSION 001 · OCEAN RESTORATION

Ocean Genesis

Investigating whether contained enzyme-assisted systems could help process collected marine plastics without releasing engineered organisms into the ocean.

WHAT MUST BE DEMONSTRATED

  • Reproducible degradation under realistic conditions.
  • Safe, measurable treatment of all breakdown products.
  • Independent ecotoxicity, containment and life-cycle evaluation.
  • Credible performance compared with established recovery options.
CONCEPT · UNVALIDATED
MISSION 002 · COMPUTATIONAL ONCOLOGY

Cancer Research — Precursor Signals

Investigating whether specific molecular and transcriptomic changes can be independently reproduced before cancer becomes clinically apparent, beginning with published colorectal-cancer research datasets.

WHAT MUST BE DEMONSTRATED

  • Suitable time-resolved or pre-diagnostic observations.
  • Replication in genuinely independent patient cohorts.
  • Control for inflammation, batch effects and sampling bias.
  • Blinded validation and appropriate ethics and clinical review.
CONCEPT · NOT A DIAGNOSTIC TOOL

SCIENTIFIC AND PUBLIC-ACCESS BOUNDARY

Built to investigate, not to overclaim.

The six specialist foundations are installed as research prototypes. Their scientific outputs are not yet integrated into a validated laboratory platform. No early cancer-detection capability, successful therapeutic outcome, real-world plastic degradation or ecological safety result is claimed. The live coordination workspace is presently private and has no public login; no personal or clinical information is displayed here.
