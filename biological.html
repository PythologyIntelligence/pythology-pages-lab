CURRENT BIOLOGICAL RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

The diseases, cell states and molecular questions we're investigating.

The goal is to investigate changes in biological state before recognised disease outcomes, using traceable evidence. These are exploratory computational programmes requiring laboratory and clinical validation.

CANCER · PRECURSOR STATES

Cancer transitions and high-risk cellular states

Bio-Symbology investigates molecular and cellular changes that may precede invasive cancer: clonal evolution, dysplasia, inflammation, DNA-repair pathways and tissue context. Pancreatic cancer is one disease-specific research track; the wider direction covers cancer types and subtypes.

Evidence boundary: Identifying a candidate precursor is not a diagnosis or a validated progression prediction.
NEUROLOGY · COGNITIVE CONDITIONS

Alzheimer's disease and neurological mechanisms

Research questions include disease-associated cellular changes, molecular pathways and competing explanations relevant to Alzheimer's disease and other neurological conditions. Migraine is also a potential mechanism-focused research problem discussed in Pythology's research direction.

Evidence boundary: No clinically established screening, progression forecast or intervention.
RARE CONDITIONS · MULTI-OMICS

Rare-disease evidence and healthy-reference comparisons

Explore heterogeneous datasets, healthy-reference transcriptomic patterns, gene expression, multi-omic networks and cross-cohort contradictions. Prototype components include evidence relationships, anomaly screening and biological hypothesis organisation.

Evidence boundary: An anomaly score is not a disease probability; independent replication is required.
COMPOUNDS · RESEARCH HYPOTHESES

Molecular perturbations and therapeutic hypotheses

Investigate how disease-state pathways might relate to published perturbation evidence, including FDA-approved compounds, and organise candidate single-agent or combination hypotheses with provenance and contradictions. This is hypothesis generation, not a medicine recommendation.

Evidence boundary: No claims of safety, efficacy, correct dosage or clinical suitability can be drawn from a computational match.

Two updated laboratory components are reserved for a separate VPS installation and verification step. Until their data sources, access controls and outputs are reviewed, this preview does not present them as live labs.

How we approach the evidence

02 / INVESTIGATE

03 / VALIDATE

Require independent evidence

Laboratory and clinical validation are necessary before any diagnostic or therapeutic conclusion.

These are computational and exploratory research programmes, not clinical tests, medical advice, validated early detection or established therapies. Real-time cell observation requires laboratory data.
EVIDENCE AND SCIENTIFIC STATUS

Biological research, without invented clinical results.

The original Pythology programme documents separate transcriptomic, multi-omic and bio-symbolic research prototypes. Those systems organise research questions and hypotheses; independent clinical performance has not been demonstrated.

REFERENCE DATA AND PROVENANCE

Healthy-reference comparisons

Gene-expression deviations and cohort-specific molecular context are screening hypotheses, not diagnosed disease. Batch effects, normal biological heterogeneity and independent replication must be investigated.

No published sensitivity, specificity or clinical validation established in this preview.
EVIDENCE-TO-MECHANISM

Pathway and perturbation hypotheses

Bio-Symbology and associated multi-omic work link candidate biological states, pathways and compound perturbations with cited evidence and contradictions. Computational relevance cannot establish therapeutic efficacy or safety.

Exploratory research prototypes — not patient-facing medical services.

Research foundations behind the disease questions.

Structured biological mechanisms

Evidence organisation and hypothesis research, not clinically validated prediction.
MULTI-OMIC BIOREACTOR ENGINE

Computational models must be checked against observed laboratory evidence.
PROTEUS MOLECULAR INTELLIGENCE

Protein sequence and variant workbench

Reproducible local calculations, sequence variation, structural context, constrained design hypotheses and evidence tracking.

Calculations do not demonstrate protein functionality or therapeutic effectiveness.
BIO-SYMBOLIC CONNECTIVITY

Compound–disease transcriptomic comparisons

A Rust-accelerated statistical research engine evaluates opposing gene-expression signatures to organise possible mechanism and compound hypotheses.

Connectivity scores do not establish treatment safety or efficacy.
