PROMETHEUS / ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

What was changing before?

Earthquakes, volcanic settings, storms, oceans and ecological conditions offer different kinds of observations. We investigate whether changes preceding major events differ meaningfully from normal variation.

WHERE THE QUESTIONS START

The event is the endpoint, not the starting point.

We look backward for testable candidate signals, then require controls and forward-looking evaluation so that apparent patterns are not mistaken for predictive capability.

CURRENT ENVIRONMENTAL INVESTIGATIONS

The research happening beneath the environmental domain.

Our work spans observational platforms, historical investigations and proposed precursor studies. They are related programmes, not evidence that an event can currently be predicted.

GEOLOGY · RESEARCH

Seismic and volcanic precursor investigations

Prometheus and the Earth-systems research work examine historical earthquake and volcanic sequences, possible changes in the weeks before events, and carefully selected quiet-period controls. Magnitude, depth, timing, source revisions and regional context matter. The question is whether a candidate signal survives when the ordinary background is included.

Evidence boundary: Research hypothesis — no validated earthquake or eruption prediction skill.
ATMOSPHERE · WEATHER LAB

Storm evolution and severe-weather evidence

Weather Lab explores weather observations, radar context and severe-weather development, including a documented retrospective Moore 2013 tornado replay and research using configured NEXRAD stations. Historical reconstruction must be kept distinct from prospective performance.

Evidence boundary: Prototype studies and observation research; official meteorological warnings remain authoritative.
NEW ZEALAND · ATLAS AND POSEIDON

Land, freshwater, ocean and conservation observations

Atlas investigates changing New Zealand environmental conditions. Related Poseidon work includes protected-waters context, marine-reserve coverage, and source-linked ocean observations; environmental streams may include freshwater, weather, ecological and coastal measurements.

Evidence boundary: Specialist observations exist separately; integration with Prometheus requires evidence and interface verification.
EARTHNET · FIELD SYSTEMS

Planetary context and Chatham Islands monitoring

EarthNet provides the broader environmental and geophysical operating picture. Separately developed Helios and EcoWatch projects have been used for community-oriented Chatham Islands monitoring. Their observations and field experience inform questions, but they are not automatically V3 results.

Evidence boundary: Operational observation systems are not themselves proof of reliable precursor forecasting.

INVESTIGATE · TEST · REMEMBER

How we approach the evidence

01 / OBSERVE

Preserve the timeline

Keep the source, region and time of each observation visible.

02 / COMPARE

Matched non-events

Challenge every interesting pattern with ordinary periods and relevant controls.

03 / EVALUATE

Prospective tests

Distinguish a retrospective observation from a tested early-warning capability.

This is exploratory environmental research, not an official hazard warning system or a demonstrated earthquake or storm prediction service.

Historical experiments — including failures

The research below was recorded in the existing Subterranean Precursor research and Weather Lab research. These are retrospective demonstrations rather than blind prospective forecasts.

NEW ZEALAND · HISTORICAL GEONET V3

Christchurch, Kaikōura and Fiordland

5 / 3 / 4

Cells classified as multi-signal in 33 Christchurch, 31 Kaikōura and 33 Fiordland replay cells respectively. The earlier near-epicentral Kaikōura signal did not survive the updated catalogue; Fiordland's earliest regional detection was distant from the eventual mainshock.

Archived retrospective analysis · recorded 10 October 2026 · spatial counts, not independent predictions
VOLCANO CONTEXT · ERUPTION-SELECTED

Nabro, Whakaari and La Palma

4 / 25 / 9

Multi-signal seismic-catalogue cells out of 49 per selected eruption replay. The centre cells for Nabro and Whakaari did not satisfy the multi-signal rule, and overlapping La Palma cells are not independent tests.

Historical event-selected replays · not validated volcano-specific sensing
PANAMA · NEGATIVE RESULT

Non-detection preserved

0 / 33

No multi-signal cells in a selected late-September to early-October 2026 regional replay. Some checkpoints were anomalous, but none met the stricter multi-signal threshold.

25 September – 9 October 2026 · not a quiet-region control or proof of safety
MOORE 2013 · RETROSPECTIVE RADAR REPLAY

A finding that came too late

+7.8 min

The first high-confidence fused tornado-supportive finding appeared 7.8 minutes after the official NWS warning. Earlier persistent radar rotation was classified 26.4 minutes before the warning, but the full detector did not show that warning advantage.

39 KTLX radar volumes · retrospective engineering test on a development case, not independent validation
