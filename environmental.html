Prometheus · Environmental

Environmental

Who this is for · Geoscience and climate researchers, conservation teams, councils, environmental agencies and organisations responsible for understanding changing conditions.

Environmental research asks what changes before an event, which signals are consistent across time and place, and which are simply ordinary variation. Early detection is the direction of the work; reliable event prediction has not been demonstrated.

What we investigate

What was changing before?

Geology

Historical and prospective seismic observations, volcanic contexts, comparisons with non-event periods and evaluation of possible precursor signals.

Atmosphere & oceans

Changes in storms, weather conditions and connected environmental observations, with careful attention to false signals and lead time.

Ecology

Environmental baselines and gradual changes that might warrant investigation before impacts become apparent.

We separate live observational systems from the currently verified, geological V3 research-engine baseline. A research candidate is not a public warning.
