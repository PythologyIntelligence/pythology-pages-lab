The research loop

Notice a pattern.
Then attack it.

Prometheus is not rewarded for finding the most correlations. He is designed to keep the relationships that continue to make sense when the easy explanations, background rate and later evidence are allowed to fight back.

01 · DISCOVER

Search across systems and time.

He examines evidence across multiple domains and lag horizons — from hours to weeks — looking for relationships worth testing rather than assuming a mechanism in advance.

02 · CHALLENGE

Ask what happens without the cause.

Matched non-cause windows and lookalike outcomes estimate how often the same apparent effect would have happened anyway. False positives stay in the record.

03 · VALIDATE

Keep the future separate from the past.

Retrospective history can suggest a hypothesis, but only prospective evidence can advance it. Chronological held-out data must also agree before confidence rises.

04 · REMEMBER

Build an inspectable relationship graph.

Cause, effect, lag, effect size, false-positive rate, lifecycle and causal status stay attached to the relationship so later evidence can strengthen it, weaken it or kill it.

OBSERVE → HYPOTHESISE → COMMIT → TEST → SIMULATE → RESOLVE → LEARN

Autonomous Decision Futures

Before the world chooses a path,
Prometheus can explore several.

When a qualifying case appears, Prometheus can freeze the evidence at T0 and commit a research-only set of alternative futures before the outcome is known. He keeps simulated branches separate from what actually happens.

FREEZE

Preserve the starting state.

Evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and the baseline forecast are sealed before later information can contaminate the case.

BRANCH

Run the alternatives.

Baseline, intervention, wait, monitor and no-action branches can be committed where they make sense. Unsupported branches remain explicitly unquantified.

RETURN

Let reality resolve the case.

When independent evidence arrives, Prometheus revisits the original case, scores what can legitimately be scored and preserves the unchosen branches as counterfactuals.

Prometheus has autonomous research authority, not autonomous operational authority. He can investigate, simulate and learn; he cannot select, authorise or execute a real-world intervention.

The simple idea

A forecast only means something
if we let it be wrong.

We did not want Prometheus to be able to explain himself only after the event. He forces the uncomfortable bit to happen first: make a clear, time-bounded forecast while the answer is still unknown.

01

Commit it.

Write down what we expect, how confident we are, what evidence we used and what competing outcome could prove more plausible.

02

Leave it alone.

The original forecast stays frozen. New evidence can be added later, but the prediction itself does not get polished after the fact.

03

Let reality score it.

When the window closes, Prometheus looks for later evidence, records the outcome and updates the calibration record — including the misses.

The original forecast stays frozen after commitment. Later outcomes are added as resolutions, not edits to the prediction.

Causal reasoning · beneath the forecast

Before Prometheus forecasts,
he can test the mechanism.

A forecast says what may happen next. The causal core asks a different question: if a specific part of the mechanism changed, what downstream effects should follow? Keeping those questions separate makes the forecast more inspectable and easier to challenge.

01

Build from evidence.

EarthNet supplies the current state. Supported causal links are declared explicitly, including their direction, strength, confidence and evidence references.

02

Test the intervention.

The compiled Rust core evaluates what changes under a chosen intervention and traces the downstream effects without confusing the scenario with an observed fact. Prometheus carries that trace forward as evidence-linked reasoning.

03

Put it on the record.

Prometheus can carry the resulting mechanism, uncertainty and provenance into the existing forecast ledger, then let later evidence decide whether the reasoning held.

EarthNet is the first operational integration of Pythology's deterministic causal core. The core does not discover causation by itself: it evaluates declared, evidence-linked mechanisms and preserves the trace for review.

New Zealand · our country-level proving ground

A useful morning brief, not another wall of data.

Prometheus produces a daily New Zealand state that brings several evidence families together before giving a short interpretation. Seismic activity, volcanic status, incoming weather and ocean context stay separate enough that we can still see where each conclusion came from.

He is deliberately careful. An earthquake concentration is described as a concentration, not quietly promoted into a precursor. Missing river telemetry remains unknown, not “normal”. Official agencies remain authoritative.

Why this matters

We would rather show the record than ask you to trust the adjective.

“Predictive”, “causal” and “intelligent” are easy words to put on a website. Prometheus is our attempt to make those claims earn their keep. He commits before the answer is known, searches for the cases that contradict him, keeps competing explanations alive, explores alternative futures and comes back when reality has something to say. The record gets more useful precisely because the misses are allowed to survive.
