RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

The question must outlive the first interesting result.

What was measured?

Check physical units, sample source, timestamps and data quality.

What might connect it?

Register candidate mechanisms and preserve provenance.

What else explains it?

Use quiet controls, known confounders and contradictory evidence.

What survives?

Retain missed hypotheses and demand independent outcomes.

Scientific progress requires more than impressive demonstrations. A research result should be repeatable, falsifiable and interpreted within the domain where it was measured.
