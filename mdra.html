Why these systems belong together

Risk moves between systems.

Severe weather can become infrastructure disruption. Environmental stress can become supply pressure. Conflict can become an energy, shipping or humanitarian problem. Space weather can disturb systems far below the ionosphere. MDRA preserves each evidence chain while allowing the relationships between those chains to be tested through retained history.

The architecture does not promote correlation into causation. It gives Prometheus the timestamps, provenance, revisions and epistemic boundaries needed to test whether an apparent relationship survives competing explanations.

The shared reasoning plane

Prometheus gets the histories.
Not just the dashboards.

The three operating systems are now the evidence foundation. The next integration gives Prometheus read-only access to their machine interfaces so it can test lagged relationships across physical, environmental and geopolitical state.

That is where the architecture becomes more than three strong products: weather against logistics and unrest, ocean conditions against marine pressure, solar activity against downstream system effects, and any other relationship that earns attention through retained evidence rather than a good story.

NOT YET A CLAIM

Cross-domain relationships begin as hypotheses. Prometheus must test them against time, geography, baselines and competing explanations before anything is promoted.

A deterministic architecture, not a deterministic universe

The boundary stays visible.

MDRA is deterministic where the system can be deterministic: acquisition, timestamps, provenance, transformations, scoring rules and retained state. Interpretation remains interpretation.

OBSERVED

What a source actually reported

Original timestamps, location, source identity, quality and retained evidence.

DERIVED

What a transparent rule calculated

Indices, departures from baseline, severity transforms, deterministic triage and other reproducible computations.

INFERRED

What analysis suggests

Causal hypotheses, geopolitical synthesis and other interpretations that remain challengeable.

PREDICTED

What should happen next

Prospective expectations committed before outcomes are known and later resolved against reality.

MDRA systems are research and decision-support systems in live validation. They do not replace official warnings, responsible authorities, domain experts or authorised operational intelligence.
