01 / Ocean restoration

🌊 Ocean Genesis

Could contained biological-enzyme treatment help process collected marine plastic without creating additional harm to ocean ecosystems?

  • Investigate published plastic-degrading enzyme evidence.
  • Evaluate seawater stability and chemical breakdown products.
  • Explore contained collection and treatment concepts.
  • Require independent ecological and engineering validation.

02 / Computational oncology

🧬 Cancer Research — Precursor Signals

Can reproducible molecular changes associated with cancer help generate testable early-disease research hypotheses?

  • Begin with published colorectal cancer transcriptomics.
  • Investigate statistical confounding and negative controls.
  • Test candidate signals in independent datasets.
  • Do not confuse anomalies with clinical screening.
