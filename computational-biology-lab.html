RESEARCH MISSIONS

Questions worth studying, not confirmed discoveries.

CANCER PRECURSOR RESEARCH

Where do molecular disease states begin to change?

Investigate early cellular transitions, cancer subtypes, healthy-reference expression, cohort context, contradicted hypotheses and disease-specific validation requirements.

OCEAN GENESIS

Can contained biology address collected marine plastics responsibly?

An exploratory question about safe, contained enzyme-based processing of collected marine plastic. It is not proof of a working intervention or permission for environmental release.

Public read-only showcase. No private datasets, internal APIs, patient records, laboratory controls or write operations are exposed.
