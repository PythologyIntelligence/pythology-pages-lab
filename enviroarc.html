One family · two operational views

Clean on the surface.
Serious underneath.

EnviroArc does not add another map. EarthNet stays global. Atlas stays national and operational. Poseidon now has its own marine-reserve map and evidence dossier, backed by the shared Atlas marine pipeline.

The shared discipline is provenance, freshness, evidence class, change detection and causal traceability. A live observation, a model estimate and a derived anomaly should never look like the same kind of evidence.

GEONET V3 · HISTORICAL NZ SEISMIC TESTS

Christchurch, Kaikōura, Fiordland

5 / 3 / 4 multi-signal cells in each replay

Multi-signal classifications occurred in 5 of 33 Christchurch cells, 3 of 31 Kaikōura cells, and 4 of 33 Fiordland cells. These are spatial counts, not independent predictions.

  • Christchurch's early pattern survived adding GeoNet, but shifted in extent and duration.
  • The previously reported near-epicentral Kaikōura signal did not survive the updated catalogue.
  • Fiordland's earliest regional detection was distant from the eventual mainshock.

VOLCANO CONTEXT · ERUPTION-SELECTED REPLAYS

Nabro, Whakaari, La Palma

4 / 25 / 9 multi-signal cells out of 49 each

Seismic-catalogue patterns were identified in all three eruption-selected historic windows. That does not demonstrate volcano-specific sensing.

  • Nabro's centre cell did not reach multi-signal.
  • Whakaari's centre cell did not either; many qualifying cells were in the wider Bay of Plenty.
  • La Palma's preceding pattern covered overlapping cells that cannot count as independent tests.

The result we keep rather than hide.

From signal to mechanism

Watch what changed.
Then ask what it could change next.

EnviroArc is being built around connected Earth-system mechanisms rather than disconnected data feeds: atmosphere ↔ ocean ↔ cryosphere ↔ ecosystems ↔ human exposure.

That lets EarthNet move beyond plotting sea ice, salinity or current speed and instead preserve the evidence needed to test chains such as ice loss → freshwater input → density change → circulation response → regional impacts.

BOUNDARY

Mechanism chains are hypotheses to test against evidence, not permission to turn correlation into causation.

Evidence before confidence

The boundary stays visible.

EnviroArc is useful only if the system remains explicit about where a claim came from, how fresh it is and whether it was observed, modelled, derived or inferred.

OBSERVED

What a source actually reported

Original timestamps, location, source identity, quality and retained evidence.

MODELLED

What a model estimates

Forecasts, reanalysis and other model products remain visibly separate from observations.

DERIVED

What a transparent rule calculated

Baselines, anomalies, rates of change, persistence and other reproducible computations.

INFERRED

What analysis suggests

Mechanisms, competing explanations and scenario implications that remain challengeable.

EnviroArc systems are research and decision-support systems in live validation. They do not replace official warnings, responsible authorities, domain experts or authorised operational intelligence.
