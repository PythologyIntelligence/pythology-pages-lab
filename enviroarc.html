One family · four specialist systems

Clean on the surface.
Serious underneath.

EnviroArc gives EarthNet the planetary view, Atlas New Zealand depth, Poseidon protected waters and Weather Lab evidence-led storm analysis. The marine research pipeline is shared with Atlas; Weather Lab operates its own native severe-weather research backend.

The shared discipline is provenance, freshness, evidence class, change detection and causal traceability. A live observation, a model estimate and a derived anomaly should never look like the same kind of evidence.

PROMETHEUS · SPECIALIST RESEARCH LABORATORIES

Study what happens before.

Beyond monitoring the world as it is, our research laboratories investigate how environments change before major events. Follow each lab for its live research screens, historical evidence, negative findings and validation methods.

01 / SUBTERRANEANGEOLOGICAL PRECURSOR RESEARCH

What changes before the Earth moves?

Subterranean studies earthquake-catalogue patterns and volcanic-region context, comparing emerging signals with historical replays, quiet controls and regional differences. The aim is to test whether any precursor holds up across faults, places and time.

02 / EXTREME WEATHERATMOSPHERIC PRECURSOR RESEARCH

What changes before a storm develops?

Extreme Weather Lab examines radar storm structure alongside separate global and New Zealand environmental precursor screens. Time-gated replays, official advisory context and careful testing seek repeatable patterns without mistaking a research signal for a warning.

Both laboratories are experimental. Identified patterns are research candidates, not validated earthquake, eruption, tornado or cyclone predictions. Official agencies remain the source of public warnings.

EnviroArc systems are research and decision-support systems in live validation. They do not replace official warnings, responsible authorities, domain experts or authorised operational intelligence.
